## Why Amway Prewash Wins

- **Solvent-based formula** — 70–90% active cleaning agents; Shout's #1 ingredient is water (a filler, not a cleaner)
- **No scrubbing or soaking** — penetrates on contact; Shout requires rubbing in and waiting
- **Removes set-in stains** — works even after stains have been washed and dried; Shout doesn't claim this
- **Handles oil-based stains** — lipstick, ink, motor oil; enzymes like Shout's can't dissolve petroleum-based substances
- **Aerosol precision** — drives formula into fabric fibers; Shout's trigger spray sits on the surface

## Where Shout Has the Edge

- **Lower upfront price** (~$5–$7 vs. $18.50)
- **Widely available** at any retail store
- **Enzyme-based** — effective on protein/starch stains (food, blood) when fresh

---

**Bottom line:** Shout looks cheaper until you do the math — 30 sprays and still failing vs. 5 sprays and done. Amway Prewash's concentrated solvent formula works on contact, no rubbing required, even on stains that have already been through the dryer. The chemistry isn't close.
